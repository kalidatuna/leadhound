# Changelog

## 0.5.0 (unreleased)

**Free is now free for real.** Everything you do yourself has no limits and needs no account: every source, all your
matches, the local business finder, website checks, follow-ups, sending from your own accounts, and AI drafts with your
own key or a local model. Pro is now the hosted leadhound AI, automatic search with email alerts, and reply detection.

New
- **Follow-ups**: leads that haven't answered come back after a few days (2–14, your choice) with a short follow-up in
  their language. Up to 2 by default; "Stop following up" ends it.
- **Reply detection** (Pro): checks your connected inbox, read-only, and moves leads that answered to Replied.
- **What works**: reply rates per source on Today, with tips that state their numbers.
- **Proof of your work**: one line per past project; each draft adds the one that matches the lead.
- **Alerts**: desktop notifications when a search finds new matches in the background; an email of the best new
  matches after each automatic search (Pro). Automatic searches now show up in the app while they run.
- **Himalayas** source: remote contract roles (not full-time jobs) for your top 3 skills.
- **Add lead** by hand, and a **browser extension** (`extension/`) that sends the page you're on to leadhound.
- **Demo mode**: `leadhound demo` opens the app with made-up sample leads in its own folder, sending off.
- **`leadhound trends`**: what clients asked for lately, as a Markdown table.

Better
- Header on one line: language, theme and Quit moved into a ⋯ menu. Tabs scroll on phones.
- **Dark mode**, following your computer or chosen in the menu.
- New hound logo and favicon.
- Searches show a chip per source (searching, done with a count, failed); the raw log moved behind Details.
- Opening a lead with no message writes the draft by itself.
- Header lines up with the Leads screen; checkboxes in the app's green; stage chips on one scrolling row.
