# Product Hunt and Indie Hackers

## Product Hunt

- **Name:** leadhound
- **Tagline (60 chars):** Find freelance clients who are asking for help right now
- **Topics:** Freelance, Open Source, Productivity, Sales
- **Gallery:** `site/social-preview.png` first, then `docs/img/today.png`, `docs/img/leads.png`,
  `docs/img/compose.png`, `docs/img/today-dark.png`, `docs/img/phone.png`. Add `docs/img/demo.gif` or the promo video.
- **Description:**
  leadhound watches public places where people ask for freelance help (Freelancer.com, Reddit [Hiring] posts, Hacker News,
  GitHub bounties, remote contract roles, job boards) and finds local businesses with website problems you can fix. Every
  lead gets a score with the reasons listed, and a first message in the client's language. Follow-up reminders, reply
  rates per source, 11 languages. Free and open source, runs on your computer.
- **Maker comment:** reuse the Show HN first comment, shorter and warmer. End with a question.
- Launch at 00:01 Pacific on a Tuesday–Thursday. Tell people who already use it a day before; ask for honest comments,
  not upvotes.

## Indie Hackers

Post in "Show IH" or write a milestone post. Indie Hackers readers like numbers and honesty, so write about the
business side:

**Title:** I made my lead-finding app free for everything users do themselves. Here's why.

Outline:
1. What leadhound is (2 sentences, GIF).
2. The first pricing: 3 searches a day, top 10 matches, sending locked. Why it felt wrong for an open-source tool:
   anyone can patch it out, and it punished exactly the users who would recommend it.
3. The new split: free = everything you do yourself; Pro = things that run for you or cost money (hosted AI, automatic
   search with alerts, reply detection).
4. What you'll measure next (trial starts, conversion), and a promise to post the numbers in a month.
5. Ask: "If you sell an open-source tool, where did you draw the free/paid line?"
